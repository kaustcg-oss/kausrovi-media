Regulatory and leadership signal

How the signal works (rules, not opinions): Red a CDRH device warning letter dated in the window, or 10 or more Class I recall records.       Method and limits

      • Recall counts are openFDA device/enforcement product records by report date, matched on the company name; one recall event can produce several records. The exact query for each company is linked from its count.
      • openFDA data was last updated 2026-09-30, so the last days of the window are not yet included.
      • Warning letters: only letters we could open on fda.gov are listed. FDA’s list cannot be searched completely from here, so “none found” does not mean none were issued.
      • Leadership changes: confirmed in company press releases or SEC filings. A search, not a complete review of filings.
      • Excluded from the ranking: WuXi AppTec (contract research and manufacturing, not a device maker); Johnson & Johnson and Thermo Fisher (not classified as medical-device companies by either source). Medline (supplies) would rank about 9th.
