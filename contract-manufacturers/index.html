Contract manufacturers: who is registered with FDA, where, and how big

Choosing or auditing a contract manufacturer starts with knowing who they are, where they operate and how large their footprint is. This beta page lists contract manufacturers from FDA’s public establishment registration and listing database, ranked by size of their public FDA footprint. It is a directory built from public records. It is not a quality ranking, a rating or a recommendation.

How the ranking works

  • What is counted: records in openFDA device/registrationlisting whose establishment type is “Manufacture Medical Device for Another Party (Contract Manufacturer)”, grouped by the owner or operator name FDA holds. One record is one registered facility–product listing. The count measures how many contract-manufacturing listings a firm has registered, so it reflects breadth and scale of what is registered. It does not measure quality, capacity, price, lead time or regulatory standing.
  • Firm names are matched on a name term (shown in the table), so related entities with the same term can be included. Counts can change when firms update their registrations.
  • Why not a “best” ranking: a fair quality ranking needs inspection, audit and performance data that is not public in a form that can be compared. We will add the neutral public signals that exist (inspection and warning-letter records, recall records) as separate columns, never merged into one score.

How to use this

  • Shortlisting: find out which suppliers operate in the country and region you need, and how many listings they have.
  • Diligence: confirm the exact legal entity and site for the product you are buying; FDA registration is not an approval or a quality certificate.
  • Next step: check the firm’s own website, request its audit reports and certifications, and look up its FDA inspection and recall records.
